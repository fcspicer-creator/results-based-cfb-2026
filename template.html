How it works

How SpicerMetric turns results into rankings. Wins earn points, losses cost points, and opponents earn their strength.

Points from wins − penalties for losses + eligible unbeaten bonus
Wins build your résumé

Every Division I win starts with 1 point. Beating a team with better results earns more. More wins help too.

Every loss costs points

Losing to a weaker opponent costs more. A close loss still costs points—there is no bonus for a “good loss.”

Unbeaten bonus: wins over FBS only

Any undefeated team can earn this bonus. Only FBS wins increase it; any loss removes it.

Small credit for how and where

Road wins earn a little extra. Larger wins help modestly, but the margin bonus stops growing at 28 points.

Opponents earn their strength

We use everyone’s results to judge opponents, repeating the calculation until the scores settle. No team starts with a ranking advantage.

Conference strength is earned separately

Conference scores use interconference results and never add to team points. No prior-season baseline is used.

Conferences

FBS and FCS conferences ranked together by games against other Division I conferences. Every result helps determine opponent strength.

Everyone is connected

All interconference results are solved together. Head-to-head results matter alongside the rest of each conference’s schedule.

No reputation advantage

Every conference starts at a neutral 50, with equal stabilization for small samples. Results determine strength—not polls or reputation.

No double credit

Conference scores never affect team points or opponent quality. Team scores already account for opponent results.

Every interconference Division I game counts once, including FBS–FCS games. More games provide more evidence: wins help and losses hurt, adjusted for the other conference’s network strength. Games within a conference, non–Division I games, margins, and venue do not affect this conference score.

Higher scores mean stronger results in this network; 50 is neutral. Scores are not percentages or win probabilities and can extend beyond 0–100. Average opponent strength is weighted by games played. Average team résumé is shown for context only and does not determine the conference order. Independents enter the network as individual teams, not a shared conference. Sparse schedules and disconnected groups limit how confidently scores can be compared. All scores recalculate with each Sunday update.

Notre Dame provision: Notre Dame receives a bid if it finishes in the CFP committee’s top 12. This projection uses our FBS ranking in place of the committee’s.

Selection rules & projection notes

Our rankings project today’s field. Each automatic-bid conference’s highest-ranked eligible team is its projected champion, not a confirmed winner. Remaining spots follow our rankings. Blank rounds await winners; no game winners are predicted.

The ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions receive automatic bids. The highest-ranked team from the American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 and Sun Belt receives another. Seven at-large places complete the field, or six when Notre Dame qualifies through its provision. Selection rank sets the seed order; an automatic qualifier below the top 12 enters at the bottom. The top four seeds receive byes. First-round pairings are 5–12, 6–11, 7–10 and 8–9; the bracket does not reseed.

These are model projections, not the official committee field or conference standings. They rebuild with every successful Sunday update at 10 a.m. Mountain, through January 31, 2027. During postseason they remain a model-based “who would qualify now” comparison, not a live results bracket.

Selection rules & projection notes

Our rankings project today’s field. Each automatic-bid conference’s highest-ranked eligible team is its projected champion, not a confirmed winner. Remaining spots follow our rankings. Blank rounds await winners; no game winners are predicted. This field uses the separate FCS rankings from FCS Top-25.

Eleven conference champions receive automatic bids: Big Sky, Coastal Athletic, Ivy, Missouri Valley, Northeast, Ohio Valley, Patriot, Pioneer, Southern, Southland and United Athletic. Thirteen at-large teams complete the field. Sixteen teams are seeded; the top eight receive byes. Unseeded placements here avoid first-round conference rematches, but do not reproduce the NCAA’s geographic placement process.

MEAC and SWAC have no automatic bids. We reserve the projected MEAC champion and both projected SWAC division winners for their separate postseason commitments; other eligible teams remain available for at-large selection, subject to actual schedule availability. These reservations use our rankings, not official clinching scenarios.

Mercyhurst, New Haven, West Florida and West Georgia are excluded during reclassification. Florida A&M is excluded because of its 2026 postseason ban. Eligibility assumptions are specific to 2026.
